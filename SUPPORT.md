# Getting help

## 🩸 Need blood urgently?

**GitHub is not an emergency channel.** Contact your hospital or a licensed blood bank now. Use the
LiquidLyf app to post a request, but don't wait on it alone.

Never pay or accept money for blood. Donation is free. A "donor" who asks for a travel fee or any
other payment is very likely a scammer; report them in the app.

## I use the app

GitHub is where the app is built. It isn't the place for help with your account, so please don't share
your phone number or other personal details here.

| You want to… | Do this |
|---|---|
| Report a user (scam, harassment, fake request) | In the app, open their request or profile and tap **Report**. You can also block them |
| Delete your account | In the app: **Settings → Delete account**. No longer have the app? See [liquidlyf.github.io/delete-account](https://liquidlyf.github.io/delete-account) |
| Find the verification or password-reset email | Check **Spam** and **Promotions**. It comes from `noreply@liquidlyf-app.firebaseapp.com`. Nothing after 2 minutes? Tap **Resend** on the Verify email screen, or ask for a new password-reset email |
| Get help with your account or the app | Email **liquidlyf@gmail.com** |
| Download or update the app | [liquidlyf.github.io/download](https://liquidlyf.github.io/download) |
| Read the privacy policy | [liquidlyf.github.io/privacy](https://liquidlyf.github.io/privacy) |

## I want to report a problem in the app

- **A bug:** open a [bug report](../../issues/new/choose). Remove names, phone numbers and locations
  from screenshots first.
- **A security problem:** please report it privately, as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Something that makes the app unsafe** (for example, a way scammers could misuse it): open a
  **Safety** issue, without personal details. If it's about a specific person, report them in the app instead.

## Organizations

Running a blood-donor club, an NGO or a hospital and want to work with us? Email **liquidlyf@gmail.com**,
or start a Discussion in **Partners & NGOs**.

## Response times

LiquidLyf is run by volunteers. We try to answer emails and Discussions within a few days. Reports of
abuse in the app are handled first.
