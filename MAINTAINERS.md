# Maintainers

The people who review, merge and release LiquidLyf. See [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) for what each
role means and how to join.

Contact the maintainers at **liquidlyf@gmail.com**, or mention `@liquidlyf/maintainers` on GitHub.

## Current

| Role | Who | Areas |
|---|---|---|
| Lead maintainer | The project founder | Everything: Android app, backend, releases, accounts |

## Open roles: we're looking for you

| Role | What it involves |
|---|---|
| **Co-maintainer** | Review and merge pull requests, approve releases, hold a second copy of the project's account access |
| **Medical advisor** | A doctor or medical student who checks eligibility rules, blood compatibility and safety wording |
| **Bangla reviewer** | A native speaker who reviews translations for clear, natural wording |
| **Moderation lead** | Handles reports about users in the app, ideally from a partner blood-donor organization |

Interested? Start a Discussion, or email **liquidlyf@gmail.com**.
