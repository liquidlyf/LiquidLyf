# Trademark policy

The code of LiquidLyf is free software under the AGPL (see [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md)). The
**name and logo are not**. This page explains why, and what you can and can't do with them.

## Why

LiquidLyf handles blood requests and donors' phone numbers. People must be able to trust that an app
called "LiquidLyf" is the real one, built from this repository by its maintainers. A copy with the same
name and icon could collect donors' phone numbers or ask them for money. So the name and logo stay
with the official app, while the code stays free for everyone.

## What the marks are

- the name **LiquidLyf™** (an unregistered trademark);
- the LiquidLyf heart-and-drop logo, in every colour and size, including the logo files in
  [`branding/`](branding/) and the app's launcher icon.

The official app is the one published by the LiquidLyf project at
[github.com/liquidlyf/LiquidLyf](https://github.com/liquidlyf/LiquidLyf/releases) and
[liquidlyf.github.io](https://liquidlyf.github.io), and any store listing linked from there. It uses the
app ID `io.github.liquidlyf`.

Other names and logos in the app belong to their owners and aren't covered by this policy or by the
AGPL. For example, the "G" on the **Continue with Google** button is a trademark of Google LLC, used as
Google's [sign-in branding guidelines](https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines) ask.

## You may, without asking

- Use the name to talk about the project: in articles, reviews, talks, tutorials and research.
- Say that your project is "based on LiquidLyf" or "a fork of LiquidLyf", as long as your own name is
  the main one and it's clear you aren't the official app.
- Use the unmodified logo next to such text to link to this project.
- Share unmodified official builds as they are, for example the APK from our GitHub Releases.

## You must, if you distribute or host a modified version

- Use a **different name and icon** that can't be confused with LiquidLyf.
- Use a **different app ID** (`applicationId`). `io.github.liquidlyf` and anything starting with it are
  reserved for official builds.
- Use **your own** Firebase project, backend, signing key and update feed. Don't point your app at the
  LiquidLyf backend or at our `update.json`.
- Use **your own** support contact, privacy policy and website.
- Point the app's **About → Source code** link at your own source code (the AGPL requires this).
- Keep the line **"Based on LiquidLyf — https://github.com/liquidlyf/LiquidLyf"** on your About screen,
  or wherever your version shows its legal notices. The licence requires it (AGPL section 7(b), see
  [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md)). This credit is the one place you must name LiquidLyf; it gives you no
  other right to use the name or logo.
- Never suggest that your app is official, endorsed by, or connected with LiquidLyf unless we have
  agreed in writing.

## Ask us first

- Co-branding, for example "LiquidLyf for <your organization>".
- Any other use not covered above, such as the logo on merchandise or event material.

We are usually happy to say yes to non-profit, community use.
